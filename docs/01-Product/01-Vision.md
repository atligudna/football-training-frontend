# Football Planner

**Product Vision**

Version: 1.0  
Status: Draft  
Last Updated: 9 July 2026

---

# Purpose

Football Planner is a modern coaching platform built for football coaches.

The goal is to simplify how coaches plan, organise, execute and review football training sessions.

Instead of using notebooks, paper sketches, PDFs and multiple disconnected applications, Football Planner provides one complete workspace for every stage of the coaching process.

---

# Vision Statement

> Football Planner should become the most intuitive and visual football coaching platform available.

The application should allow coaches to spend less time planning and more time coaching.

Planning a training session should feel as natural as thinking about it on the football pitch.

---

# Target Users

Football Planner is designed for coaches at every level.

Examples include:

- Youth coaches
- Academy coaches
- Goalkeeper coaches
- Professional coaches
- Football schools
- Clubs with multiple age groups

The application must work equally well for one coach managing one team and for an academy with many coaches working together.

---

# Core Philosophy

Football coaches do not think in databases.

Football coaches think in:

- Players
- Coaches
- Football pitches
- Training sessions
- Drills
- Stations
- Time

Football Planner should always reflect this mental model.

The application should never expose unnecessary technical concepts to the user.

---

# What Makes Football Planner Different

Football Planner is not simply another drill library.

It is a complete coaching workspace.

The application combines:

- Session planning
- Drill management
- Visual football pitch design
- Timeline planning
- Coach collaboration
- Mobile coach mode
- Printing
- Future AI assistance

Everything should work together seamlessly.

---

# Product Goals

The application should help coaches:

- Plan better sessions
- Save planning time
- Reuse previous sessions
- Share sessions with other coaches
- Visualise training
- Organise multiple coaches
- Run training sessions directly from a phone or tablet

---

# Long-Term Vision

A coach should be able to create an entire football session without touching paper.

The complete workflow should be:

Plan →

Visualise →

Coach →

Review →

Reuse

Football Planner should become the coach's primary workspace throughout the entire training cycle.

---

# Design Principles

Football Planner should always be:

- Visual
- Fast
- Intuitive
- Flexible
- Reusable
- Mobile friendly
- Coach focused

Every design decision should support at least one of these principles.

---

# Future Vision

Future versions may include:

- AI generated training sessions
- Automatic drill recommendations
- Animated drills
- Attendance tracking
- Season planning
- Match preparation
- Individual player development
- Parent communication
- Video integration
- Statistics and analytics

These features should build on the same core architecture without requiring major redesign.

---

# Success

Football Planner is successful when a coach can:

- Create a complete session in minutes.
- Run the entire training session from a mobile device.
- Reuse sessions across seasons.
- Coordinate multiple coaches during the same training session.
- Spend more time coaching and less time managing administration.

---

# Our Vision

Football Planner is more than a planning application.

It is a complete visual coaching platform built around the way football coaches actually think and work.
