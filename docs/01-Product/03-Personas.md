# Purpose

Football Planner is designed for several different types of football coaches.

Each persona represents a real coaching environment with different goals, responsibilities and challenges.

Every major feature should support at least one persona.

---

# Persona 1

Works with one or more youth teams.

Plans sessions every week.

Reuses previous sessions.

Wants to visualise training.

Uses a phone during training.

Works with one or more assistant coaches.

Needs to adapt quickly when player numbers change.

# Persona 2

Works with several coaches.

Large player groups.

Multiple pitches.

Club methodology.

Coordinates sessions.

Works inside a football academy.

Typical examples

- Elite youth football
- 50–120 players
- Multiple coaches
- Multiple pitches

### Goals

- Coordinate coaches
- Build consistent training methodology
- Share sessions
- Assign responsibilities

### Challenges

- Large player groups
- Several coaches
- Multiple pitches
- Different ability levels

### Uses Football Planner for

- Multi-pitch planning
- Coach assignments
- Shared Drill Library
- Timeline
- Session Templates
