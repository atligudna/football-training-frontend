# Purpose

This document describes how different users experience Football Planner during a normal day.

The application should support natural coaching workflows rather than forcing users to learn software.

---

# Journey 1

## The Modern Coach

### Monday Evening

The coach opens Football Planner.

Dashboard immediately shows:

- Next training
- Previous session
- Quick Actions

The coach clicks:

New Session

↓

Creates Wednesday's training.

↓

Chooses:

2 Pitches

↓

Pitch A

Standard Training

↓

Pitch B

Station Training

↓

Adds drills

↓

Assigns coaches

↓

Saves session.

Time spent:

Approximately 10 minutes.

---

### Wednesday

30 minutes before training.

Dashboard shows

Today's Session

↓

Equipment Ready

↓

Coaches Assigned

↓

Training Ready

The coach walks onto the pitch.

---

### During Training

Coach opens Coach Mode.

The phone shows

Current Drill

↓

Remaining Time

↓

Player Focus

↓

Next Activity

The coach never needs paper.

---

### After Training

Coach finishes session.

↓

Writes short notes.

↓

Rates session.

↓

Marks two drills as favourites.

↓

Session automatically becomes reusable.

# UX Principles

Football Planner should always answer the following questions immediately.

Where am I?

What am I doing?

What happens next?

What should I focus on?

The interface should never leave the user uncertain.

---

# Guiding Principle

Football Planner should feel like an experienced assistant coach standing beside you.
